UNDERSTANDING COLLEGE COSTS

Plan for Your Investment.

The cost of college goes beyond tuition. Housing, food, books, transportation, and personal expenses can all be part of your college budget.

01
TUITION Varies

Tuition can vary based on residency, campus, academic level, and program.

02
HOUSING + FOOD Varies

Housing and meal plan costs depend on the options a student chooses.

03
OTHER EXPENSES Plan Ahead

Students should also consider books, supplies, transportation, and personal expenses.

Costs can change from year to year. Students should always check Penn State's current information when planning their college budget.
