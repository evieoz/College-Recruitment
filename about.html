ABOUT PENN STATE

A Legacy of
Possibility.

For more than 160 years, Penn State has been helping students turn their curiosity into real opportunities and make a positive impact on the world.

OUR STORY

Rooted in a Stronger
Tomorrow.

Founded in 1855, Penn State grew from its beginnings as an agricultural college into a major public research university.

Today, Penn State gives students opportunities to explore academics, research, leadership, service, athletics, organizations, and experiences beyond the classroom.
